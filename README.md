**Proof**
- Start here: [ai-operated-growth-engine](https://github.com/nunomnferreirapt/ai-operated-growth-engine), the AI operating system, as built and sanitized. About 20 production skills, a weekly chain of autonomous loops, a human approval gate on everything customer-facing. It is how demand generation, paid media, CRM and the documentation portal ran side by side, without a dedicated marketer for each.
- GeoDin, 2023 to 2026: subscriptions from 1% to ~90% of external sales (expected for 2026); external sales from flat to a 17% CAGR 2024 to 2026; NRR ~110% with zero churn among new logos.
